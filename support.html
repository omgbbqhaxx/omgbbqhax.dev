Bitfight

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to move the falling blocks, swipe down to drop faster and double tap to rotate. Line up blocks of the same color, then drop a killer block on them to clear the whole group. Red attacks, green heals, yellow boosts and blue sends extra blocks to your rival.

How do multiplayer matches work?

Multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. Make sure you are signed in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then choose multiplayer in the app.

Is Bitfight free?

Yes. Bitfight is free to download and play.

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

Game progress is stored on your device. If you delete the app, local progress may be lost. Contact us and we will help if we can.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at hello@omgbbqhax.dev with your iPhone model and iOS version, or post in our Discord or r/bitfight.

flooor.fun

What is flooor.fun?

flooor.fun is a live market on the Base network. You can bid on the floor, sell instantly, and holders who sign in daily share in the rewards generated by sales.

Do you have access to my wallet or funds?

No. flooor.fun is non-custodial. We never see your private keys or recovery phrase, and we can't move your funds. Never share your recovery phrase with anyone, including people who claim to be our support team.

My transaction failed or is stuck.

Check the transaction on a Base block explorer using your wallet address. Most failures happen because of low gas balance or an auction state that changed. If you still need help, email us with the transaction hash.

How do I turn off notifications?

Email us with your wallet address and we will turn off notifications for that address.
