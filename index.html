Bitfight

Puzzle game · iPhone

A colorful competitive block puzzle. Four colors, four powers and one grid between you and your rival. Stack, chain and blast your way to victory in quick one-on-one matches.

  • Free to play on iPhone
  • 1v1 battles with Game Center
  • Easy to learn, hard to master

flooor.fun

Web app · Base

A live marketplace on the Base network. Collectors can bid on the floor, sell instantly, and holders who sign in daily share in the rewards generated by every sale.

  • Live floor bids and instant sales
  • Daily sign-in rewards for holders
  • Self-custody: users keep control of their own wallet
Visit flooor.fun Support Privacy

BBlock

Building system · block-chain.world

A single-component interlocking concrete brick system. Bricks stack dry without mortar, rotate 90° for clean corners and are reinforced with steel rods. Cast with our 3D-printed molds.

  • No mortar required
  • One brick shape for walls and corners
  • Ideal for garden walls, planters and small structures
