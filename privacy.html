Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how OMGBBQHAX LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("OMGBBQHAX", "we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use our products: the Bitfight mobile game, the flooor.fun web application, the BBlock website (block-chain.world) and this website, omgbbqhax.dev.

We collect as little information as possible. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Bitfight (iOS app)

What we collect

  • No account is required. Bitfight does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Game Center. If you use Game Center for multiplayer matches or leaderboards, Apple provides us with your Game Center player ID and display name so we can match you with other players. This is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Game progress such as scores and settings is stored on your device.
  • Diagnostics. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Tracking

Bitfight does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. If we ever add features that require tracking, we will update this policy and ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt first.

flooor.fun (web app)

  • Wallet address. When you connect a crypto wallet, we receive your public wallet address. We use it to show your bids, holdings and rewards. We never receive or store your private keys or recovery phrase. You stay in full control of your wallet.
  • Public blockchain data. Bids, sales, signatures and claims are recorded on the Base blockchain. That data is public by nature and cannot be changed or deleted by us or anyone else.
  • Notifications. If you turn on in-app notifications, we store your wallet address and notification preferences so we can send you updates about auctions and rewards. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers may briefly log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

Our websites

omgbbqhax.dev does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. block-chain.world may use Google Ads conversion measurement to understand which ads bring visitors to the site. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run our products, such as website hosting, Apple Game Center and blockchain infrastructure (RPC) providers. They may process data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service.

Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Support emails are deleted when they are no longer needed. We use reasonable technical measures, including HTTPS everywhere, to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your rights & data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@omgbbqhax.dev. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Bitfight: deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. Game Center data is managed in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center.
  • flooor.fun: email us with your wallet address to delete your notification preferences and any other off-chain data. On-chain transactions cannot be deleted.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. flooor.fun is intended only for adults of legal age in their country. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
